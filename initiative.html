TEAM AI TRIPLE E · POLICY HACKATHON 2026

Supporting teachers to use AI, not replacing the teacher.

Our proposed intervention helps Bangladesh primary teachers use generative AI for lesson preparation, classroom activities and assessment, with teacher judgment at every stage.

Experience the teacher toolkit
THE PROBLEM & RESPONSE

Why a teacher-led approach?

The proposal responds to constraints that can affect lesson planning, classroom delivery and ongoing assessment in primary education.

CHALLENGE

Classroom needs are practical and immediate

  • Teachers need time to prepare and adapt learning activities.
  • Class size, available resources and preparation time vary.
  • Generic AI answers may be inaccurate or unsuitable.
  • Digital tools cannot replace professional judgment.
OUR PROPOSED RESPONSE

A guided AI-assistance toolkit

  • Six task-focused teaching modules rather than a new AI model.
  • RCTC-based prompts using teacher-provided classroom information.
  • Written guides and future video tutorials through QR links.
  • ARR checks to keep the teacher responsible for use.
This page presents the intervention concept. Evidence, exact policy recommendations, pilot parameters and sourced impact figures must be inserted from the approved policy brief before public presentation.
PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION LOGIC

From a practical tool to institutional support

01 · TEAM INITIATIVE

Prepare and test

Finalise prompts and guidance, seek authorised teacher feedback and verify practicality.

02 · PILOT LEARNING

Improve through use

Assess usability, teacher confidence and the quality of teacher-reviewed materials, subject to permission.

03 · POLICY ADVOCACY

Recommend institutional support

Use credible pilot lessons to advocate practical teacher guidance and oversight, through the appropriate authority.
